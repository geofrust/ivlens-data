IV Lens Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

IV Lens doesn't collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your Pokémon collection and settings stay on your iPhone, and nothing about you is sent to us or anyone else.

What the app reads on your iPhone

  • Your screen, while you record it. When you start a screen broadcast to IV Lens, the app reads Pokémon GO's appraisal screen to work out each Pokémon's stats. Text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Frames are analysed in memory and thrown away; they are never uploaded.
  • Saved frames, only if you turn it on. With "Save missed frames" on, frames the scanner couldn't read are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone so you can look at them. They stay there until you delete them, and leave your iPhone only if you share them yourself.
  • A picture of each Pokémon you scan. The app keeps a small picture of the Pokémon, cropped from the appraisal screen, to show in your collection. It stays on your iPhone and is deleted with the Pokémon (the next time the app opens, so an undo can still bring it back), or all at once in Settings › Display.
  • Screenshots and recordings you pick. Images and videos you choose with the photo picker or the Files app are read on your iPhone. The app sees only the items you pick, not the rest of your library.

What it stores

  • Your collection, notes, settings and a daily backup, all on your iPhone. The backups are in the Files app under On My iPhone › IV Lens › Backups.
  • If iCloud Backup is on, your iPhone's backup includes this data. That's Apple's service, covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of it, apart from any copies you exported yourself.

What it downloads

About once a week, and when you tap Update in Settings, the app downloads game data and battle rankings from geofrust.github.io/ivlens-data, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests contain no personal information. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

When you open the Raids tab, and at most every 6 hours, the app also downloads the current raid bosses and the raid and Max Battle event calendar compiled by LeekDuck, from the ScrapedDuck project on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), and Snacknap's page of current Max Battles (snacknap.com). These requests also contain no personal information. The app makes no other internet connections.

What you share

Data leaves the app only when you choose to send it: for example sharing a CSV export, a backup or a Pokémon summary, or copying a search string. It goes wherever you send it.

Notifications

Scan banners are local notifications created on your iPhone. They don't use any push service.

Children

IV Lens collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
