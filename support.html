IV Lens Support

Help with scanning, your collection and battle rankings.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue at github.com/geofrust/ivlens-data/issues. A screenshot of what you see helps a lot.

Getting started

  1. Open IV Lens and tap Start scanning, choose IV Lens, then Start Broadcast.
  2. Switch to the game, open a Pokémon and appraise it until the three stat bars show.
  3. Hold the appraisal on screen for about a second. A banner shows the result and the Pokémon is saved.

Tap Overlay on the Dashboard to float the result over the game while you play.

Common questions

A Pokémon wasn't saved.

Keep the appraisal screen still until all three bars are filled in. Very fast tapping, an open menu or a notification covering the bars can stop it from being read. Turn on Settings › Save missed frames to see what the scanner saw.

A mark (shiny, shadow, lucky…) is wrong.

Long-press the banner and choose the fix, or open the Pokémon and change it. Marks you set yourself are never changed by later scans.

Scanning stopped by itself.

iOS ends a screen broadcast if its memory use gets too high, or when the screen locks. Start it again from the Dashboard. Settings › Diagnostics shows what happened.

Are the rankings up to date?

Game data and league rankings update once a week on Wi-Fi. To update now, go to Settings › Game data › Update game data & meta rankings.

How do I move my collection to a new iPhone?

Your iPhone's iCloud Backup includes it. You can also export a backup from Settings › Backup & import and import it on the new phone.
